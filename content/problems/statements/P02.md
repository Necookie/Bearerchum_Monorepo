## Problem Statement
A street has a row of lamps, numbered 1, 2, 3, ... from left to right. Each lamp is `1` (lit) or `0` (broken).

A lit stretch is a group of lit lamps side by side with no broken lamp between them. You repair exactly one broken lamp, making it lit. Find the longest lit stretch you can get with the best choice of repair.

Tip: repairing a broken lamp joins three parts: the lit lamps directly to its left (up to the next broken lamp or the end of the row), the repaired lamp itself, and the lit lamps directly to its right. Try every broken lamp and keep the longest result.

For example, with `1011100111101` the broken lamps are 2, 6, 7 and 12.
Repairing lamp 2 gives 1 + 1 + 3 = 5
Repairing lamp 6 gives 3 + 1 = 4
Repairing lamp 7 gives 1 + 4 = 5
Repairing lamp 12 gives 4 + 1 + 1 = 6
The best repair is lamp 12, so the answer is **6**.

## Input Format
- The row of lamps as one word, read after the prompt `Enter lamps: `

## Output Format
- `Longest lit stretch: L` on its own line, where **L** is the length of the longest lit stretch

## Constraints
- The row has 1 to 100 lamps and contains only the characters `0` and `1`.
- At least one lamp is broken. Every lamp may be broken.
- A broken lamp may be at either end of the row.
- Several repairs may give the same longest stretch.
