## Problem Statement
Print the elements of an R×C matrix in clockwise spiral order, starting at the top-left: go right along the top row, down the right column, left along the bottom row, up the left column, then repeat on the inner part.

For example, the 3×3 matrix `1 2 3 / 4 5 6 / 7 8 9` gives `1 2 3 6 9 8 7 4 5`.

## Input Format
- **R** and **C** on one line after the prompt `Enter rows and columns: `
- The program then prints `Enter the matrix:` on its own line and reads **R** rows of **C** space-separated integers.

## Output Format
- `Spiral order: …` with the elements separated by single spaces

## Constraints
- 1 ≤ R, C ≤ 100
- The matrix may be a single row or a single column.
- The matrix may be non-square.
