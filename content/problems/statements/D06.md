## Problem Statement
A shop recorded its sales for **N** days. Find the largest total of sales over any **K** consecutive days.

For example, with K = 3 and sales `2 5 1 8 2 9 1`, the best window is `8 2 9` with total **19**.

## Input Format
- **N** and **K** on one line, separated by a space, after the prompt `Enter N and K: `
- The **N** sales values on one line, read after the prompt `Enter sales: `

## Output Format
- `Best total: X`, where **X** is the largest sum of K consecutive days

## Constraints
- 1 ≤ K ≤ N ≤ 10^5
- K may equal N.
- Sales may be negative (the answer can be negative).
- Each sales value has absolute value at most 10^4.
