## Problem Statement
A map has land `#` and water `.`. Land cells that touch up, down, left or right belong to the same island (diagonals do NOT connect). Count the islands.

## Input Format
- **R** and **C** on one line, separated by a space, after the prompt `Enter rows and columns: `
- The program then prints `Enter the map:` on its own line and reads **R** lines of **C** characters each.

## Output Format
- `Islands: K`, where **K** is the number of islands

## Constraints
- 1 ≤ R, C ≤ 100
- Every character is `#` or `.`.
- The map may be all water (answer 0).
- Diagonal land cells are separate islands.
