## Problem Statement
Create a program that converts a Roman numeral to its integer equivalent. Roman numerals are represented by seven different symbols: I, V, X, L, C, D, and M.

Symbol Values:

- I: 1
- V: 5
- X: 10
- L: 50
- C: 100
- D: 500
- M: 1000

Typically, Roman numerals are written largest to smallest from left to right. However, there are six instances where subtraction is used:

- I can be placed before V (5) and X (10) to make 4 and 9.
- X can be placed before L (50) and C (100) to make 40 and 90.
- C can be placed before D (500) and M (1000) to make 400 and 900.

Your task is to read a Roman numeral string and output the corresponding integer value.

## Input Format
- A single line containing a string s, which is a valid Roman numeral, read after the prompt `Enter Roman numeral: `

## Output Format
- A single integer representing the value of the Roman numeral, printed as `Integer value: X`

## Constraints
- The length of s will be between 1 and 15, inclusive.
- The value of the Roman numeral will be between 1 and 3999.
