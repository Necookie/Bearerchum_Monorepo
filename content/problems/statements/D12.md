## Problem Statement
Print the greatest common divisor and least common multiple of **N** positive integers.

For example, for `12 18 24` the GCD is **6** and the LCM is **72**.

## Input Format
- **N**, read after the prompt `Enter N: `
- The **N** integers on one line, read after the prompt `Enter numbers: `

## Output Format
- `GCD: g` on the first line
- `LCM: l` on the second line

## Constraints
- 1 ≤ N ≤ 100
- N may be 1 (the GCD and LCM are the number itself).
- 1 ≤ each number ≤ 10^6
