## Problem Statement
Given a sentence, find the length of its longest word. Words are separated by single spaces, and the length of a word is its number of letters.

For example, in `i love programming in c` the words have 1, 4, 11, 2 and 1 letters, so the longest word length is **11**.

## Input Format
- One sentence on a single line, read after the prompt `Enter sentence: `

## Output Format
- `Longest word length: X`, where **X** is the number of letters in the longest word

## Constraints
- The sentence has 1 to 200 characters, only lowercase letters `a` to `z` and spaces.
- Words are separated by exactly one space, and there are no spaces at the start or end.
- The sentence may be a single word with no spaces.
- The longest word may be the first or the last word, and several words may share the longest length.
