## Problem Statement
The temperature was recorded on **N** consecutive days. Find the longest run of consecutive days where each day is strictly warmer than the day before. A single day counts as a streak of 1.

For example, in `20 21 22 19 20 21 23` the run `19 20 21 23` has 4 days, each warmer than the one before, so the answer is **4**.

## Input Format
- **N**, read after the prompt `Enter N: `
- The **N** temperatures on one line, read after the prompt `Enter the temperatures: `

## Output Format
- `Longest streak: L`, where **L** is the length of the longest warming streak

## Constraints
- 1 ≤ N ≤ 10^5
- Temperatures are integers and may be negative.
- Equal temperatures break a streak.
- The longest streak may be at the very end.
