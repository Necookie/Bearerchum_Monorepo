## Problem Statement
A student returns **N** library books. Each book has its own number of days **D** it was kept. A book kept for 7 days or less has no fine. A book kept longer is fined 5 pesos for every day beyond the 7th, but the fine for one book is never more than 50 pesos. Print the total fine.

For example, for the books `3 9 7 20 12`, the fines are:
3 days: 0
9 days: 2 × 5 = 10
7 days: 0
20 days: 50 (13 × 5 = 65 is more than 50)
12 days: 5 × 5 = 25
So the total is **85**.

## Input Format
- The integer **N**, read after the prompt `Enter number of books: `
- **N** integers **D** on one line separated by spaces, read after the prompt `Enter days kept: `

## Output Format
- `Total fine: T`, where **T** is the total fine in pesos

## Constraints
- 1 ≤ N ≤ 100
- 1 ≤ D ≤ 365
- A book kept exactly 7 days has no fine.
- Every book may be on time, so the total may be 0.
- A book kept 17 days or more is fined exactly 50 pesos.
