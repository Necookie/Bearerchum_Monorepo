## Problem Statement
A water company bills a household for the **U** cubic meters of water it used this month. Each of the first 10 cubic meters costs 15 pesos. Every cubic meter beyond the first 10 costs 25 pesos. Print the total bill in pesos.

For example, if **14** cubic meters were used:
The first 10 cost 10 × 15 = 150 pesos
The other 4 cost 4 × 25 = 100 pesos
The bill is 150 + 100 = **250**

## Input Format
- The integer **U**, read after the prompt `Enter cubic meters used: `

## Output Format
- `Bill: B`, where **B** is the total bill in pesos

## Constraints
- 0 ≤ U ≤ 1000000
- **U** may be 0, in which case the bill is 0.
- **U** may be 10 or less, in which case no cubic meter is charged at 25 pesos.
