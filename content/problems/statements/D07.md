## Problem Statement
Check whether every `(`, `[`, `{` is closed by the matching bracket in the right order. Ignore other characters.

For example, `{[()()]}` is balanced, but `([)]` is not: the `)` arrives while `[` is still open.

## Input Format
- The expression on one line, read after the prompt `Enter expression: `

## Output Format
- `Result: Balanced` or `Result: Not Balanced`

## Constraints
- 1 ≤ length ≤ 10^4
- A closing bracket may come first.
- Brackets may be left open at the end.
- A text with no brackets at all is balanced.
