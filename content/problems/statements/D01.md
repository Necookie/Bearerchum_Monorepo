## Problem Statement
Count the vowels (a, e, i, o, u) and consonants in a line of text. Case does not matter. Ignore digits, spaces and symbols.

For example, `Programming 101!` has the letters `Programming`: 3 vowels (o, a, i) and 8 consonants.

## Input Format
- The text on one line, read after the prompt `Enter text: `

## Output Format
- `Vowels: V` on the first line, where **V** is the number of vowels
- `Consonants: C` on the second line, where **C** is the number of consonants

## Constraints
- 1 ≤ length ≤ 1000
- The text may contain no letters at all (both counts are 0).
- `y` is a consonant.
- Uppercase and lowercase letters count the same.
