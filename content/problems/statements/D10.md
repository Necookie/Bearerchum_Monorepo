## Problem Statement
Rotate an N×N matrix 90° clockwise.

For example, the matrix

```
1 2 3
4 5 6
7 8 9
```

becomes

```
7 4 1
8 5 2
9 6 3
```

## Input Format
- **N**, read after the prompt `Enter N: `
- The program then prints `Enter the matrix:` on its own line and reads **N** rows of **N** space-separated integers.

## Output Format
- `Rotated:` on its own line, then the **N** rows of the rotated matrix, numbers separated by single spaces

## Constraints
- 1 ≤ N ≤ 100
- N may be 1.
- Values are integers and may be negative.
