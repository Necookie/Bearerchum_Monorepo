## Problem Statement
A theater has **R** rows of seats. The first row has **S** seats, and every row after it has 2 more seats than the row in front of it. Print the total number of seats in the theater.

For example, with **10** seats in the first row and **4** rows, the rows have 10, 12, 14 and 16 seats, so the theater has:
10 + 12 + 14 + 16 = **52** seats

## Input Format
- The integer **S**, read after the prompt `Enter seats in first row: `
- The integer **R**, read after the prompt `Enter number of rows: `

## Output Format
- `Total seats: T`, where **T** is the total number of seats in all **R** rows

## Constraints
- 1 ≤ S ≤ 1000
- 1 ≤ R ≤ 1000
- **R** may be 1, in which case only the first row exists.
