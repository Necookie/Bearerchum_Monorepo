## Problem Statement
Count the paths from the top-left cell to the bottom-right cell of a grid, moving only RIGHT or DOWN. Cells marked `#` are blocked; cells marked `.` are open.

For example, an open 3×3 grid has **6** paths.

## Input Format
- **R** and **C** on one line after the prompt `Enter rows and columns: `
- The program then prints `Enter the grid:` on its own line and reads **R** lines of **C** characters each.

## Output Format
- `Paths: P`, where **P** is the number of paths

## Constraints
- 1 ≤ R, C ≤ 100 (answers get huge — fine in Python)
- The start or the end may be blocked → 0
- A 1×1 open grid has exactly 1 path.
