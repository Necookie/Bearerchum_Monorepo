## Problem Statement
Round a whole number **N** to the nearest multiple of 10 and print the result.

Look only at the last digit of **N**. If it is 5 or more, round up to the next multiple of 10. If it is 4 or less, round down by dropping it to 0.

For example, **247** ends in 7, which is 5 or more, so it rounds up to **250**.

## Input Format
- The integer **N**, read after the prompt `Enter number: `

## Output Format
- `Rounded: R`, where **R** is **N** rounded to the nearest multiple of 10

## Constraints
- 1 ≤ N ≤ 1000000000
- A last digit of exactly 5 rounds up.
- If **N** is already a multiple of 10, it does not change.
- When **N** is less than 5, it rounds down to 0.
- Rounding up may add a digit (when **N** ends in several 9s).
