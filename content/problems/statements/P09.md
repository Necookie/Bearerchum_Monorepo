## Problem Statement
Given two whole numbers **A** and **B**, go through every number from **A** up to **B** in order. Add the first number, subtract the second, add the third, subtract the fourth, and keep alternating until **B** has been used. Print the result.

For example, with **A** = **3** and **B** = **7**, the numbers are 3, 4, 5, 6 and 7, so the result is:
3 - 4 + 5 - 6 + 7 = **5**

## Input Format
- The integer **A**, read after the prompt `Enter start: `
- The integer **B**, read after the prompt `Enter end: `

## Output Format
- `Alternating sum: S`, where **S** is the alternating sum of the numbers from **A** to **B**

## Constraints
- 1 ≤ A ≤ B ≤ 1000000
- **A** may equal **B**, in which case the only number is added.
- The result may be negative.
