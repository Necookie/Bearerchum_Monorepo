## Problem Statement
A cheap calculator has a broken display: it cannot show the digit 0. When a number is entered, every 0 is skipped and the remaining digits move together, keeping their order. Given the entered number **N**, print the number the display shows.

For example, if **N** is **40507**, the display skips both zeros and shows the digits 4, 5 and 7, which is **457**.

## Input Format
- The integer **N**, read after the prompt `Enter number: `

## Output Format
- `Display: D`, where **D** is **N** with all of its 0 digits removed

## Constraints
- 1 ≤ N ≤ 1000000000
- **N** always has at least one nonzero digit, so the display is never empty.
- **N** may have no zeros at all; the display then shows **N** unchanged.
- Zeros may appear anywhere after the first digit: in the middle, at the end, or in long runs.
