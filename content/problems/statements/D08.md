## Problem Statement
Find the word that appears most often in a sentence (case-insensitive). On a tie, choose the alphabetically first word.

For example, in `the cat and the dog and the bird` the word `the` appears 3 times, more than any other word.

## Input Format
- The sentence on one line, read after the prompt `Enter sentence: `

## Output Format
- `Most frequent: word (count)` with the word in lowercase

## Constraints
- Words are separated by spaces and contain only letters.
- Ties are possible.
- `Hello` and `hello` are the same word.
- The sentence has at least one word and at most 1000 characters.
